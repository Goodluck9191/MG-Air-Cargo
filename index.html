About MG Air Cargo

Your Trusted Partner in Cargo & Logistics

MG AIR CARGO is a trusted logistics and transportation company based in Dar es Salaam, providing reliable cargo handling, freight forwarding, and delivery services to businesses and individuals across Tanzania and beyond. From the moment your shipment leaves our hands to the moment it arrives, we treat every parcel with the same care as if it were our own.

Customer satisfaction sits at the center of everything we do — backed by a professional team, modern tracking systems, and a five-star reputation earned one delivery at a time.

  • Licensed & professional cargo handling

  • Nationwide & international reach
